A stage manager gives status every thirty seconds and nobody in the room finds
it excessive.

"Standby camera two." "Camera two, ready." "Take two."

Three sentences, no adjectives, no interpretation. Everyone hears the same
thing at the same time, and everyone knows what happens next. Nobody has ever
described this as micromanagement.

Put the same cadence on a program and people get uncomfortable.
